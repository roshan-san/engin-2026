# Trial Cycles are the only path into a team (besides Invites)

Engin's premise is "prove it through real work", so there is no way to apply to a Role directly: a Role is only a listing that Trial Cycles attach to, and a person becomes a Member by accepting an Offer created by a _passed with offer_ Verdict (or by a Founder's Invite). Direct role applications and the founder-side "add participant to team" action were removed because they let people onto teams without a Verdict and without the person's consent.

## Consequences

- Offers never expire; only the Founder can withdraw them. When a Role reaches its Headcount, pending Offers for it are withdrawn, not-yet-started Trial Cycles for it are cancelled, and running ones finish but can no longer produce _passed with offer_.
- One attempt per person per Trial Cycle; a second chance means joining a later Trial Cycle for the same Role.
