# engin

Startups hire by running paid Hackathons: people apply, the Founder picks Participants, they work real Cards for a week or two, and the Founder decides whom to hire.

## Hiring

**Application**:
One person's request to take part in one Hackathon, and the record of everything the Founder does about them from then on.
_Avoid_: Submission, entry

**Applicant**:
A person with an Application who isn't yet a Participant.

**Participant**:
An Applicant the Founder accepted. They work the Hackathon's Cards once it starts.
_Avoid_: Builder

**Declined**:
An Application the Founder turned down, or that wasn't accepted by the start. A Declined Applicant can be accepted again while the Hackathon is Open.

**Shortlist**:
The Founder's ★ on a Participant they're considering hiring. The first Decide step.

**Walkthrough**:
The Founder's call with a shortlisted Participant about their work. The second Decide step, done once, and needed before Hired.

**Decision**:
The Founder's final word on a Participant: Hired or Not this time. Made once, after the Hackathon ends, and it ends that Participant's Decide steps.
_Avoid_: Verdict, outcome
