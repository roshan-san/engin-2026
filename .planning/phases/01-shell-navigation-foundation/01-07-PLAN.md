<objective>
Build the mobile half of the shell (SHELL-01), exactly as D-14–D-17 describe:
- a persistent bottom tab bar (Inbox · My Pulses · Startup · Discover);
- a Startup bottom sheet that is the mobile twin of the sidebar's Focused-Startup section;
- a slim top bar whose avatar opens an account sheet;
- the Notifications | Threads toggle on Inbox.

Purpose: the shadcn Sidebar's own mobile mode would give a hamburger sheet, which is the wrong design (research Pitfall 1). These are hand-built with the raw `Sheet` and `Tabs` primitives, reusing plan 01-06's hooks, nav constants and components.
Output: 5 mobile components, AppShell mounting them below `md`, a safe-area utility, and screen titles.
</objective>
