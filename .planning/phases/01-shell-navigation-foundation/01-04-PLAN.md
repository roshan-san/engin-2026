<objective>
Build the new URL scheme and its layout composition (SHELL-02, SHELL-03, SHELL-06), per ADR 0006 and D-10:
- The pathless `_shell` layout chooses the app shell or the public header by sign-in state.
- The pathless `_authed` layout gates sign-in-only screens.
