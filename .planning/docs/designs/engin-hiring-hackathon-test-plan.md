## Affected Pages/Routes
- /s/$slug/hiring: create a Trial Cycle as a draft, publish it (credit or payment), see the credit balance
- /pricing: Free vs Pro (₹999/mo, ₹9,999/yr), per-hackathon ₹2,999 / ₹1,499, INR checkout
- /discover (Explore) and /startup/$slug: only published hackathons appear; drafts never do
- /s/$slug/trials/$trialCycleId: apply/join, the IP acknowledgment tick, the 5-entry cap message
- /u/$username: verdicts show the issuing startup

## Key Interactions to Verify
- Publish with a Pro credit on /s/$slug/hiring: the hackathon goes open and appears in Explore
- Publish with no credit: goes to Dodo checkout (INR), and after paying it auto-publishes
- Pay after the draft's dates passed: the credit shows in the balance and the draft stays a draft with a "pick new dates" prompt
- Claim a launch code: the balance goes up by 1, and reusing the code shows an error
- A founder or team member tries to apply/join their own startup's hackathon and is blocked
- Joining a 6th live hackathon shows the cap message (no Pro bypass)

## Edge Cases
- Double-click Publish or publish from two tabs: exactly one credit spent
- The same Dodo renewal delivered twice, or active+renewed in the same period: one credit
- Pro founder with 3 banked credits renews: still 3
- Pro cancelled: banked credits usable until period end, then gone
- Stealth startup tries to publish: asked to go public first
- A contributor accepts a team invite mid-trial: verdict recorded, Score unchanged

## Critical Paths
- Founder: create draft → publish (credit or pay) → contributors apply/join → start → close with verdicts → offer accepted → Score updates
- Existing lifecycle tests (start/cancel/close/verdict/offer/boards/threads) pass unchanged via the createTrial helper
